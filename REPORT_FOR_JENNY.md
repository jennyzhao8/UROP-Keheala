## Session 4 changes (May 6–7)

### 1. Logit table — substantive redirect

Your `generate_logistic_regression_table` was the right idea but had two issues that mattered for the conclusions readers would draw:

- **Quasi-separation in the type-I and type-II columns.** With ~31 and ~21 events respectively against ~9 patient covariates plus 7–9 active province dummies, the unpenalized fit returns coefficients like extrapulmonary $\beta = -13.1$ (SE 398) for type II — a textbook separation tell. The docstring claimed Firth penalization but the code was vanilla MLE, so reporting those columns at face value would mislead. The fix could have been Firth (`firthlogist`) or a "—" flag for non-estimable cells, but neither read well in a paper-facing table.
- **Two-clinic concentration not absorbed.** With ~43% of missing-in-TIBU events sitting at clinics 0 and 115, the missing column was implicitly modelling "those two clinics + everyone else." The apparent male / clinic-size / record-age effects were partly that.

I made three changes:
1. **Restricted the table to the missing-in-TIBU outcome.** False-positive and false-negative rates are too low to identify a multivariate model with province FE, and false positives are dominated by audit-snapshot artifacts anyway (§5). The marginal evidence on FP/FN now lives in Tables 3 and 4 only.
2. **Added a second column excluding clinics 0 and 115.** Direct sensitivity check rather than relying on the regression to disentangle.
3. **Switched cells to average marginal effects in percentage points** (delta-method SEs). Easier to read for clinical/non-econometrics audiences than log-odds.
4. **Cluster-robust SEs by clinic** — within-clinic correlation matters here.

Result: the apparent male coefficient ($+2.6$ pp) and clinic-size coefficient ($-1.0$ pp) collapse to near-zero once the two clinics are excluded, while the record-age effect (mostly mechanical reporting lag) survives but halves in magnitude. None of the patient or clinic structural covariates is a robust predictor of missingness. This is `tblSI_logit_errors.tex`, generated by the rewritten `generate_logistic_regression_table`.

### 3. §4 restructured into when / where / who

This was the bigger change. The old structure (crosstab → clinic → patient → over-time) buried the two strongest substantive findings — the COVID-era spike and its concentration at two clinics — at the back, and made readers do their own integration. The new structure leads with them:

- **§4.1 Crosstab** — unchanged.
- **§4.2 When did errors occur?** — formerly "Error rates over time." Pure temporal narrative: COVID-era missing-in-TIBU spike, paper-date sensitivity check (Appendix A confirms it's outcome-driven, not reporting-driven). No clinic-specific facts here anymore.
- **§4.3 Where did errors occur?** — formerly "Error rates by clinic characteristics." Introduces the two clinics (IDs 0 and 115) as the spatial counterpart of the §4.2 temporal finding, motivates the two-panel layout, and describes how the error rates flatten under the exclusion. Closes with a one-sentence pointer to the multivariate appendix.
- **§4.4 Who experienced errors?** — formerly "Error rates by patient characteristics." Inherits the two-panel layout from §4.3 without re-introduction.

The `\label` keys are now `sec:errors_when`, `sec:errors_where`, `sec:errors_who`, and the §1 Overview prose is updated to describe this order ("ask, in order, *when* errors occurred, *where* they occurred, and *who* experienced them").

### 4. Two-panel sensitivity in Tables 3 and 4

`generate_error_by_clinic` and `generate_error_by_patient_characteristics` are both parameterised with the side-by-side panel. The left panel is the full classifiable sample; the right panel applies `~clinic_id_num.isin(COVID_CLINICS)` where `COVID_CLINICS = {0.0, 115.0}` is a new module-level constant. The `gated=True` versions (Appendix B) automatically inherit the same two-panel layout, so the gated and exclusion sensitivities can both be read off there.

A few implementation notes:
- The clinic-size median is computed once on the full sample (`df.drop_duplicates("clinic_id_num")["tibu_patients"].median()`), so "Large clinic" / "Small clinic" mean the same thing in both panels.
- Rows are kept even when zero in one panel, so the visual structure is symmetric.
- The save-box pattern is preserved; the body `\input` site uses `\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}` with `\footnotesize` cells, which fits the 17-column width on a portrait page.
- The right-panel column header reads "Excluding clinics 0 and 115" (initially "Excl.\ 2 COVID clinics"; renamed at Erez's request to make the exclusion explicit).
